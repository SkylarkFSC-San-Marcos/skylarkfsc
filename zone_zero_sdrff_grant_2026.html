Zone Zero Fence & Gate Grant Application

Home / Programs / Zone Zero Grant

Improve Your Home's Wildfire Resilience

Skylark Fire Safe Council may provide funding assistance for replacing combustible fencing and gates adjacent to homes with non-combustible, wildfire-resistant materials.

  • Reduce ignition risk within Zone Zero.
  • Improve home hardening against embers.
  • Support community wildfire resilience goals.
  • Advance Firewise and defensible space objectives.
Important: Please upload clear photos showing the existing fence and/or gate. Applications without photos cannot be evaluated.
