Skylark Fire Safe Council Receives $3,000 Grant from San Diego Regional Fire Foundation

The Skylark Fire Safe Council is pleased to announce that it received a $3,000 community grant from the San Diego Regional Fire Foundation in June 2026.

This funding will help support the Council's ongoing efforts to improve wildfire preparedness, community education, and fire safety initiatives within the Skylark neighborhood and surrounding areas.

Skylark Fire Safe Council is grateful to the San Diego Regional Fire Foundation for investing in local wildfire resilience and recognizing the importance of neighborhood-based fire prevention efforts. Grants such as this enable volunteer-led organizations like Skylark Fire Safe Council to expand outreach, strengthen preparedness programs, and continue building a safer community for all residents.

How the Grant Supports Our Mission

The grant will support activities that advance the Council's mission of:

  • Promoting wildfire awareness and preparedness
  • Supporting defensible space and vegetation management education
  • Enhancing community engagement and outreach
  • Strengthening partnerships with local fire agencies and emergency preparedness organizations

Skylark Fire Safe Council is committed to ensuring that these funds are used responsibly and effectively to benefit the community.

The Council would also like to thank its volunteers, board members, residents, and community partners whose continued support makes this work possible.

Together, we are helping create a more resilient and fire-adapted Skylark community.

Residents interested in learning more about wildfire preparedness or volunteering with Skylark Fire Safe Council are encouraged to contact the Council, attend upcoming community events, or subscribe to community updates.
