Sea Chen and Warm Sunrise Charitable Foundation Pledge Support to Skylark Fire Safe Council

The Skylark Fire Safe Council is honored to recognize a generous $500 donation pledge from Sea Chen and the Warm Sunrise Charitable Foundation in February 2026 in support of our efforts to enhance wildfire preparedness and strengthen community resilience within the Skylark neighborhood.

This pledge reflects a shared commitment to strengthening local communities and promoting public safety through wildfire awareness, education, and risk-reduction initiatives. Support from charitable organizations and community partners helps Skylark Fire Safe Council continue expanding programs that encourage residents to prepare for wildfire emergencies and protect their homes, families, and surrounding natural environment.

Skylark Fire Safe Council is sincerely grateful to Sea Chen and the Warm Sunrise Charitable Foundation for their support of the Skylark community. This generous pledge helps advance our mission of building a safer and more resilient neighborhood through education, outreach, and wildfire mitigation efforts.

How This Pledge Supports Our Mission

The donation pledge will help support activities that advance the Council's mission of:

  • Promoting wildfire awareness and preparedness
  • Supporting defensible space and vegetation management education
  • Enhancing community engagement and outreach
  • Strengthening neighborhood readiness and wildfire resilience

As California communities continue to face wildfire challenges, local partnerships remain critical to improving preparedness and reducing risk. Contributions such as this donation pledge help support the Council's ongoing work to engage residents, share practical preparedness information, and foster a culture of readiness throughout the community.

The Skylark Fire Safe Council deeply appreciates the confidence and encouragement shown by Sea Chen and the Warm Sunrise Charitable Foundation. Their support strengthens our ability to continue serving residents and promoting wildfire resilience across the Skylark community.

About Skylark Fire Safe Council

The Skylark Fire Safe Council is a California nonprofit organization dedicated to reducing wildfire risk through community education, preparedness initiatives, and collaboration with local agencies and partner organizations. The Council works to help residents better understand wildfire hazards, implement defensible space practices, and strengthen neighborhood resilience.

Thank You

Skylark Fire Safe Council gratefully acknowledges the $500 donation pledge made in February 2026 by Sea Chen and the Warm Sunrise Charitable Foundation. Their support contributes directly to our mission of protecting lives, homes, and the natural environment through wildfire preparedness and community engagement.
