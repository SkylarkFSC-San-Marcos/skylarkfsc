Skylark FSC Receives IRS 501(c)(3) Nonprofit Approval

Nonprofit Status

Skylark FSC Inc. Approved by IRS as a 501(c)(3) Nonprofit Organization

This important milestone strengthens Skylark FSC's ability to serve the community, reduce operating costs, and access more resources for wildfire preparedness and community resilience.

IRS 501(c)(3) ApprovalSkylark FSC Inc.Community Resilience

Skylark FSC Inc. is pleased to announce that it has been approved by the Internal Revenue Service as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. This approval marks a major step forward in our mission to support wildfire preparedness, community safety, and long-term resilience for the Skylark neighborhood.

With 501(c)(3) status, Skylark FSC can operate more efficiently and reduce the cost of maintaining the organization. This recognition also opens the door to additional resources, partnerships, grant opportunities, and community support that can help expand our fire safety education, preparedness outreach, and neighborhood risk-reduction efforts.

"This IRS approval is an important milestone for Skylark FSC. It gives us a stronger foundation to serve the community, pursue more resources, and continue building a safer and more resilient neighborhood together."

Zimin Liu, Treasurer, Skylark Fire Safe Council

This achievement reflects the dedication, persistence, and teamwork of many people who helped prepare the organization for this step. Skylark FSC extends its sincere appreciation to the board members for their guidance, commitment, and support throughout the process.

We would especially like to recognize Zimin Liu for his dedication to the application process and his significant contributions in document preparation, financial support, and filing efforts. His work helped move the application forward and played an important role in reaching this milestone.

What This Means for Skylark FSC

The approval strengthens Skylark FSC's ability to serve residents by improving operational efficiency and expanding access to nonprofit resources. As the organization grows, this status will help support continued collaboration with community members, local partners, public agencies, and other organizations committed to wildfire safety.

Skylark FSC will continue focusing on practical preparedness, community education, defensible space awareness, and neighborhood resilience. The new nonprofit status provides a stronger structure for sustaining these efforts and building broader support for the Skylark community.

About Skylark Fire Safe Council

The Skylark Fire Safe Council is a California nonprofit organization dedicated to reducing wildfire risk through community education, preparedness initiatives, and collaboration with local agencies and partner organizations. SFSC works to help residents better understand wildfire hazards, implement defensible space practices, and strengthen neighborhood resilience.

Thank You

Skylark FSC gratefully thanks the board members for their commitment and support, with special appreciation to Zimin Liu for his dedication, document preparation, financial support, and contributions throughout the IRS 501(c)(3) filing process.
